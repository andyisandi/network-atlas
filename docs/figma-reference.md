# Network Atlas — design source

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/OKKIs2mwrCNLesyIJyvXRU/Network-atlas?node-id=4-108

Team: Andi’s Own Team. Page: design (`0:1`). Frame: interface (`4:108`), 1728 × 1117.

Typography: Fragen DEMO Light, 32 px and 5% tracking for NETWORK ATLAS; Space Grotesk Regular, Light and Bold for UI; Space Mono Regular for CATEGORIES and SENIORITY, 12 px and 10% tracking; Halisa DEMO Light for search and the level selector. All four families are bundled locally. Fragen, Halisa and Space Grotesk were available in the user’s installed font library; Space Mono was obtained from the Google Fonts repository. The Space families’ OFL licenses are included.

Palette: black #000000, white #FFFFFF, secondary gray #BCBCBC, range-track gray approximately #717171, and the self marker #FF0D0D. The newer user-supplied colour notation supersedes the original blue ramp. The latest gradient-circle reference supersedes the segmented rendering and reverses relevance shading. Category hue families remain in sidebar order. Dim/desaturated to bright/saturated endpoint pairs are: Brand & Graphic #25372F / #25F4A0; Creative Leadership #253735 / #20EEE2; Motion & Media #253438 / #27DCFF; Product & Digital #28303D / #4B9FFF; Talent & Recruiting #33293B / #B457FF; Marketing & Strategy #392B3C / #EE49FF; BU Community #3B2E34 / #FF61B7; Other Disciplines #3B2E30 / #FF5A77. Brightness and saturation increase with relevance. In All Connections, multiple category hues mingle through broad, asymmetrically placed radial colour fields clipped to one circle. Each person has a deterministic individual arrangement. Filtered views retain one category hue, and hover/selection still fades to yellow. The texture is rendered sharply at ordinary distances; near-camera depth-of-field blur is applied afterward. The latest sphere reference supersedes the red self marker: Andi is an orange/violet shaded volume with soft edges, a subtle halo and time-varying internal gradient fields.

The live Figma UI assets remain in dist/assets. The two curved celestial images were style references and are excluded from the live map; their source copies and the old toggle SVG are archived in docs/reference-assets. Every displayed relationship line joins actual captured endpoints. The switches are now CSS controls with one outlined thumb at left when off and one white-filled thumb at right when on, easing in and out over 420 ms.

The source frame has no authored animation timeline. The requested idle motion and camera travel are implemented procedurally in dist/galaxy.mjs: a deterministic loosely spaced three-dimensional volume (at least 90 world units between resting points, up to ±1,416 in depth), bounded orbital drift, perspective projection, cursor-anchored camera travel, depth-separated background stars, smooth camera easing and decaying momentum after wheel, drag, Command-drag rotation and pinch input. Camera yaw and pitch apply to every dot, relationship endpoint and background star; labels remain readable in screen space. Command-drag orbits around the current view center on the focus plane, preserving camera distance. Ordinary drag pans in screen space at any orientation; wheel and trackpad pinch zoom under the cursor. Pitch limits avoid flipping through the poles. Recenter returns to the original orientation, while Fit preserves orientation. Command plus arrow keys provide an accessible rotation alternative. The initial camera fits every mapped person and centers the self marker. Pause motion freezes the idle phase; reduced-motion mode holds positions still and makes camera transitions immediate.

Relationship patterns describe actual captured endpoints: solid lines join Andi to each direct contact; dashed lines join contacts with a verified mutual relationship. No second- or third-degree classification is inferred. Hovering a dot or name, or keyboard-focusing a name, transitions dot colours, line colours, rings and unrelated-dot dimming together over 360 ms, preserving continuity when reversed. The self sphere does not initiate yellow highlighting. Breadth-first traversal of verified contact edges, excluding Andi’s universal hub, gives increasingly muted yellow for more distant paths. Stronger relevance has a steeper radius scale, and foreground dots gain Gaussian blur only inside a 260-unit near-camera zone. Overview and middle-distance dots stay sharp. The profile card displays title and company directly below the name, extracted only from explicit captured headline wording or existing structured fields; unavailable fields remain marked as such. Categories, switches and the relevance threshold animate opacity over 420 ms, including when idle motion is paused. Unselected categories use light grey; selection is white, and a one-pixel right border restores the sidebar separator.

The latest dot-size reference informs a radius of 0.9 + 55 × (relevance / 100)^4.4 before perspective and screen bounds. The cloud reference informs gentle, category-coloured connector curves between verified endpoints; its text and colour bar are not reproduced. Idle orbit amplitudes are 14–31 world units in x, 65% of that in y, and 8 in z, with angular speeds 0.22–0.36. Clicking pins the selected graph trail while hover can temporarily emphasise another dot. The right panel enters over 400 ms. All these transitions respect reduced-motion preferences.

The expanded-cloud reference informs an adjacent 3D volume, not an invented graph. External nodes enter only from observed second-degree profiles, have current score >60, and are connected only by recorded connection-list or mutual-card evidence. Dense fan links are thin and stagger their dash phase; an individual hovered/inspected bridge returns to full emphasis. Further graph steps fade more strongly. All dropdown menus now use black fill, white outlines and white type, with listbox keyboard navigation. The static original self ellipse is archived in docs/reference-assets.

Hover now reveals a compact adjacent preview through the same 420 ms opacity transition, with no camera displacement. Pointer exit includes a short grace period to reach the prospect cloud. Click pins and eases the preview into the full adjacent 3D layout.

The self sphere uses a brighter gold/orange/lilac palette, a 0.7 radius multiplier at the overview, and perspective scaling by camera depth. It is a scene object at the 3D origin, composited between farther and nearer people instead of overlaid on the scene.
