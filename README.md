# NETWORK ATLAS — Andi Zhang

A private static website for Andi Zhang’s LinkedIn network, restyled from the design page of Network atlas in Andi’s Own Team on Figma. Built with dependency-free HTML, CSS, Canvas and JavaScript ES modules.

This public repository contains the current source and real professional-network snapshot, published with the owner’s explicit approval. It starts from a clean source snapshot and does not contain the private Site’s Git history, raw LinkedIn captures, credentials or Site project binding. The separately hosted atlas retains its owner-private access settings.

## Preview

Run `python3 -m http.server 4173 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory dist` from this folder, then visit `http://127.0.0.1:4173`. Serve over HTTP; ES modules and the data fetch do not work reliably by opening the HTML as a file.

## What is included

- 584 unique profiles captured from the end-to-end signed-in LinkedIn connection list on November 16, 2026. LinkedIn reports 603; 19 are unavailable in the returned list.
- 251 unique verified cross-links from six mutual-connection searches and Adam Kelley’s visible mutual link. This is a partial mutual graph, not evidence of no other links.
- Nonexclusive category estimates for every captured profile, with the matched headline evidence retained per membership. 98 people have multiple categories. All 29 captured BU ties appear in BU Community. Recruiter vacancy descriptions and employer names alone do not create discipline memberships. Broader design and insufficient-detail cases are marked. The review uses the captured details, not a fresh live-profile audit. See `docs/category-review.csv`; regenerate with `node review-categories.mjs`.
- Headline-based estimates of job functions, industry fit, opportunity signals and seniority. No sensitive personal traits enter the scoring.
- 29 headline BU ties; 2 explicitly indicate alumni status. The rest retain unknown alumni status.
- Organization sizes sourced for Trivium Interactive, Stoltze Design Group and Workshop/APD (4 profiles). Other sizes remain unknown.

## Relevance

Default weights: industry 30, function 30, BU alumni 15, organization size 10, opportunity signal 15. Scores are the weighted mean of signals on a 0–100 scale. Unknown factors contribute neutral 50%; the interface separately reports the weight covered by available signals. Scores are not hiring likelihoods. Organization size matches the selected preference; bigger organizations are not automatically better. All weights zero gives neutral 50.

`dist/model.mjs` defines the rules. `node tests.mjs` checks edge integrity, scoring, unknowns, student and recruiter seniority, manual-override reversibility, and size preferences.

## Interaction

The initial view fits the whole galaxy. Drag to pan; hold Command (⌘) and drag to orbit horizontally and vertically around the center of the view; wheel or pinch to move the camera into the region under the cursor; double click a person to travel closer. A loosely spaced three-dimensional volume spans roughly 2,800 world units in depth, with at least 90 units between resting dot positions. Perspective creates parallax; foreground blur begins only within 260 world units of the camera, leaving the overview and middle distance sharp. Dots visibly drift in idle state. Pan, rotation, wheel and pinch gestures retain a short, exponentially decaying momentum after release. Rotation preserves orbit distance, uses the current focus plane as its pivot, and stops short of the vertical poles. Recenter restores the original orientation; Fit keeps the current orientation. All wheel input zooms, including horizontal wheel events. Each category has its own hue sampled from the supplied color notation reference: green, turquoise, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, pink and coral in sidebar order. Within each hue, higher relevance becomes brighter and more saturated; weaker relevance is darker and desaturated. In All Connections, multiple memberships appear as softly mingled, irregular colour fields inside a crisp circle; choosing a category uses its hue. The radius scale ranges from tiny points to pronounced larger discs, following the dot-size reference. Idle drift is 14–31 world units at 0.22–0.36 angular speed, slightly more visible than before. Pause motion freezes the scene. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. `Home` recenters on Andi; `+` and `-` zoom; arrow keys pan a focused map; Command plus arrow keys rotate it; `/` focuses search; `Escape` closes the profile card. Connections view provides a keyboard-accessible list. Click a person to see title and company beneath the name, followed by the score breakdown, known links, sources, and editable context. Role and employer use explicit captured headline wording or existing structured data; unavailable fields are marked rather than invented. These details are not live-verified. Solid lines connect Andi to direct contacts; dashed lines connect contacts who have a verified relationship with each other. Hover a dot or name, or focus a name with the keyboard, to fade its dot and incident links into bright yellow over 360 ms while unrelated dots dim; further verified paths use a steeper yellow fade (line alpha 1 → 0.42 → 0.15 → 0.05 → 0.018), excluding Andi’s universal hub. Dense discovery fans use thinner, quieter links; hovering or inspecting one prospect restores its bridge to full yellow. Clicking pins that person’s yellow graph trail until another person is selected or the panel is closed, even after the pointer leaves or idle motion is paused. Hovering another person adds a temporary yellow dot without replacing the pinned trail. The right panel eases in from the edge. Andi’s self marker is a shaded orange/violet sphere with continuously morphing colour fields and a soft halo. Its animation follows Pause motion and reduced-motion preferences, and it does not turn yellow on hover. These paths do not classify anyone as a second- or third-degree LinkedIn connection. Category, switch and relevance-threshold changes fade nodes and relationship lines over 420 ms. Unselected categories are grey; the selected category is white. Subtle coloured curves connect actual relationship endpoints for the supplied cloud-reference aesthetic; solid and dashed patterns retain their meanings. No additional network edges are fabricated, and none of the cloud image’s text or colour bar is copied. Star contacts and add notes locally. Preferences and notes persist per browser, not across devices. JSON exports back up all data and preferences; JSON restore applies saved preferences and annotations to the current captured network. CSV exports the current filtered list. PNG exports the current map.

## Data & privacy

The deployable static bundle is in `dist/`; the Site is configured for owner-private publication. `Orbit-Network-Atlas.html` is a self-contained local copy: double-click it to open the atlas without a server. Regenerate it with `node make-standalone.mjs`. Raw browser captures are ignored by Git and excluded from the hosted bundle. No LinkedIn credentials or private contact details are included in the bundle. There is no live sync, automated outreach, profile scraping service, backend, analytics, or API key. Company source links and LinkedIn profiles open only when clicked. Fragen DEMO Light, Halisa DEMO Light, Space Grotesk and Space Mono are bundled locally; fonts do not require remote requests. See docs/figma-reference.md for the exact Figma source, asset roles, typography and palette.

Raw source capture files, if present locally, can be processed again with `node build-data.mjs`. Refreshing source data requires a new authorized capture; the page does not imply live access.
